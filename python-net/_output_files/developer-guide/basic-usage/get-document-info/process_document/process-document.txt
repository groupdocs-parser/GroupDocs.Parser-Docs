Annual Report 2025


1. Overview
Harbor Lane Provisions supplies specialty food to 1,265 independent grocers, cafes and restaurants from its
Quayside warehouse in Port Ellery. In 2025 net revenue grew 15.1% to $21.4 million, driven by the Autumn
Harvest range and by coffee from our long-standing supplier, Northwind Coffee Roasters.

2. Operations
Orders shipped rose 13.1% to 55,300 while on-time delivery improved to 97.4%. Two investments made the
difference: a third cold room for cheese and dairy, and route planning that groups deliveries by district
instead of by customer.
Warehouse and logistics
    •   Cold room 3 opened in April and holds 140 pallets at 2 C.
    •   Average pick time fell from 6.1 to 4.8 minutes per order.
    •   Four electric vans replaced diesel vans on the harbour routes.


3. The Autumn Harvest range
The Autumn Harvest range launched in September with eight seasonal lines, from apple butter and squash
soup to a spiced roast blended by Northwind Coffee Roasters. The range sold out twice before November,
and harvest lines now make up 9% of revenue.
Supplier partnerships
We buy from 27 producers, 19 of them within 200 km of Port Ellery. Every supplier signs our quality charter
and is audited once a year.
Northwind Coffee Roasters
Northwind roasts our Harbor Blend, Dockside Decaf and Lighthouse Espresso beans to order and delivers
twice a week. The supply agreement renewed in January for three more years.

4. Financial summary
Gross margin improved by 1.6 points to 32.8%, as freight costs fell and the share of own-brand lines grew.
Operating costs rose 9.8%, mostly from the new cold room and three new hires in the warehouse.

Cash and investment
Operating cash flow was $2.3 million. We invested $1.1 million in the cold room and the van fleet, and closed
the year with no bank debt.

5. Sustainability
Food waste fell to 1.2% of stock, the lowest since we began measuring. Unsold harvest produce goes to the
Port Ellery food bank every Friday.
    •   Electric vans cover 40% of delivery kilometres.
    •   All coffee is traceable to the farm through Northwind Coffee Roasters.
    •   Packaging is 86% recyclable or compostable.
6. Outlook
In 2026 we plan a second Autumn Harvest season with twelve lines, a wholesale web shop, and a regional
depot for customers north of the river.
